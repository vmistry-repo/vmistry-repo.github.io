Good to know

Clear answers before you apply.

Does photoLab guarantee acceptance?

No. photoLab is an independent preparation tool, not a government authority. Automated checks and profiles reduce common errors, but the issuing authority always makes the final decision.

Can I enter my own required size?

Yes. Enter dimensions in millimetres, centimetres, inches or pixels, then choose DPI, output format, background colour, copy count and an optional JPEG file-size target.

What if the authority requires an approved photographer?

photoLab completes the output first, then displays the applicable provider, secure-transfer, on-site-capture or confirmation notice. The app never presents its output as official certification.

Does the app contain advertising?

Yes. The free app includes Google Mobile Ads with consent controls. Banners and native ads can appear within the interface, with app-open and standard interstitial ads at suitable transitions.
