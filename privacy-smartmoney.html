1. Scope and developer identity

This Privacy Policy governs the Smart Money: Insider Tracker Android app and its product and privacy pages. References to “we” or “our” mean VMWorks Studios (VMWorks). This policy does not govern the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, Google account services, your browser, device manufacturer or app store.

The app does not create a separate developer-hosted account or send your app database to a developer-operated backend. It uses a selected email address to identify its SEC data requests. Information you voluntarily send to our support mailbox is handled as described in Section 14.

3. The SEC contact email

SEC’s public fair-access rules ask automated clients to identify themselves with contact information. Smart Money uses the email obtained during Google account selection/sign-in, or entered through the fallback when sign-in fails. It stores that email locally and includes it in the User-Agent header on filing-data requests to www.sec.gov and data.sec.gov, including background checks.

  • We do not use this stored email for marketing or send it to a developer-operated app backend.
  • SEC receives it with requests and handles server records under its own privacy practices.
  • The current Settings screen displays the sign-in email as read-only; it cannot be changed there.
  • To remove it locally or restart setup with another address, clear the app’s storage in Android settings. This also deletes other local app data.

Removing local data does not delete records already received by the SEC or your Google account.

4. Google account selection and sign-in

The app uses Google account services to let you select an email address during setup. If that process fails or is cancelled, it offers manual email entry. The current app uses the selected email for SEC access rather than creating a separate account with us.

We do not receive or store your Google password. Google’s account services may process account, device and connection information as part of their operation under Google’s Privacy Policy. Clearing Smart Money’s local data does not delete your Google account or information held by Google.

5. Network activity

The app’s filing-data client requests public information directly from these hosts over HTTPS:

  • data.sec.gov — submissions and company-facts (XBRL) APIs;
  • www.sec.gov — filing archive pages, the latest-filings atom feed, the ticker mapping file and EDGAR browse search.

SEC receives your IP address, request time, user agent including the email described in Section 3, and the requested resource. Although the watchlist is not uploaded as a list, company identifiers in requests can reveal what you follow or view. The client disables redirects and cleartext traffic. SEC’s own privacy and access policies govern its handling of these requests.

Browsing recent filings, discovering managers, opening documents, comparing financials and background monitoring can all generate SEC requests. The app caches public results to reduce repeated downloads. Synthetic demo data, where available, is separate from live data and does not trigger the SEC client.

Opening an official filing or another external link launches a browser or browser tab, where that browser and destination apply their own practices. Google account services operate separately as described in Section 4.

6. Android permissions

  • Internet and network state: used to fetch public SEC data and detect connectivity.
  • Notifications: used for filing alerts, informational notes and activity reminders. Android 13 and later require runtime permission. You can deny or revoke permission, or disable notification channels in Android settings.

The app’s filing and sharing features do not require access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone or general photo library. A shared image is generated from the app window in private cache storage and made available to the chosen recipient using temporary content access.

7. Notifications and monitoring

Setup enables background monitoring for live data. Periodic work checks SEC sources and may produce notifications for configured filing rules. The app can also issue educational notes and return-to-app reminders using locally stored activity dates. No developer push-notification backend receives your watchlist or delivery history.

Notification content may appear on the lock screen according to your Android settings. Disable permission or individual channels in Android settings to control visibility. Blocking notifications does not by itself stop data requests while the app is installed; Android’s background-data and battery controls can restrict background activity. Checks and delivery are subject to scheduling, connectivity and SEC access limits.

8. Retention and deletion

Local information is kept as needed for app features until it is pruned, cleared or the app is uninstalled. Settings → Clear downloaded data is not a full reset: it clears the HTTP cache and prunes filings older than 90 days for unfollowed entities, along with orphaned transaction and holding records in the active database. It preserves follows, preferences, the email address, alert rules and financial facts, and does not erase the other mode’s database.

To delete all local app data, use Android Settings → Apps → Smart Money: Insider Tracker → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Device wording may vary. To restart setup with another email, clear storage and reopen the app. The app declares Android cloud-backup and device-transfer exclusions for its application data.

Shared images can remain in private cache until removed by cache cleanup, Android or full data deletion. Copies sent through the share sheet are controlled by their recipients. Local deletion cannot erase records held by the SEC, Google, your browser or a sharing destination; those providers set their own retention rules.

9. Security

Smart Money uses Android’s application sandbox, app-private storage, HTTPS for SEC requests, declared backup exclusions and temporary content access for shared images. The local database and preferences are not encrypted with a separate application-managed key. Device security, screen-lock choices and operating-system protections remain important. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Children

Smart Money is a financial-information tool intended for adults, not children under 13. We do not knowingly request children’s personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to our support mailbox, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

11. This website

The Smart Money product and privacy pages are static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. Their page code does not set cookies, load remote fonts or include analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub may process ordinary hosting information, including IP address, requested page, request time and browser user agent, to deliver and secure the site under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Following a Google Play, Google privacy, SEC or other external link opens a separate service with its own practices. Email links open your chosen email application.
