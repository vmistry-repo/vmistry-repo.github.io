1. Scope and developer identity

This Privacy Policy governs the Smart Money: Insider Tracker Android app and this product website. References to “we,” “our,” or “Smart Money” mean the developer identified as VMWorks in the Google Play listing. This policy does not govern the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, your browser, device manufacturer or app store.

Smart Money does not offer user accounts and does not operate any server that receives user information. There is no backend: the app is a client that talks to public SEC endpoints.

3. The SEC contact email

SEC’s public fair-access rules ask automated clients to identify themselves with a contact email. Smart Money asks for yours during setup and stores it in app-private storage. It is attached as a User-Agent header on requests to sec.gov and data.sec.gov.

  • It is not used for marketing, profiling or any other purpose.
  • It is not sent anywhere except SEC endpoints.
  • It is never included in the filings you share.
  • Changing or removing it in Settings updates it immediately.
  • You may use any email address you control; a dedicated or disposable-style address works if you prefer not to use your primary one.

Demo mode requires no email at all and makes no network requests.

4. Advertising and consent

Smart Money includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on your region, device settings and the consent choices you make in the app, Google and its advertising partners may access or process:

  • advertising identifiers and consent signals;
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from network information;
  • device, operating-system, app and language information;
  • diagnostics, ad impressions, ad interactions and fraud-prevention signals.

This information is used to provide and measure advertising, cap ad frequency, prevent abuse and maintain service security. It is never used to identify you personally, and it is never combined with your watchlist, the filings you view, or your SEC contact email — those stay in the sections of the app described above.

Where required, a consent form is shown at first launch and can be revisited any time via the privacy options entry in Settings. Google’s handling of ad data is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and Ads and Data information.

Ad placements in Smart Money:

  • Native ads appear inside content lists (feed, watchlist, whale portfolios) and at the end of a filing’s detail page, clearly labelled;
  • App open ads may appear when the app returns to the foreground, frequency capped;
  • Interstitials may appear when leaving the app, and occasionally when sharing a filing.

A failed or unavailable ad never blocks any feature: content loads and actions complete exactly the same without it.

5. Network activity

The app contacts only the following public hosts over HTTPS:

  • data.sec.gov — submissions and company-facts (XBRL) APIs;
  • www.sec.gov — filing archive pages, the latest-filings atom feed, the ticker mapping file and EDGAR browse search.

These servers receive your IP address, request time, the app’s user agent (which includes the contact email described in Section 3) and the public resource requested. Cleartext traffic is disabled. The app never uploads your watchlist, cached data or any derived content.

Opening a filing on sec.gov launches your browser or in-app browser tab, where the destination’s privacy practices apply.

6. Android permissions

  • Internet and network state: used to fetch public SEC data and detect connectivity.
  • Notifications: used only for alerts about filings from companies or managers you follow. Granted only after you tap a prompt in the app and can be revoked anytime in system settings.

No other permissions are requested. The app does not access location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.

7. Notifications and monitoring

When you follow an entity and enable monitoring, the app checks SEC endpoints from the background at a modest interval and posts a notification when a new filing matches. Checks happen only while the feature is enabled and stop when you disable it or clear your follows. Notification content contains filing titles and company names — never your email address.

8. Retention and deletion

Watchlist, preferences, the contact email and cached filing data remain on the device until you delete them by clearing data in Android settings, using “Clear cache” in Settings, or uninstalling. Android backup and cloud data extraction are disabled for this app, so this data is not copied to Google’s servers through backup.

Content shared through the Android share sheet (snapshots, text) is handled by the destination app or service; we cannot delete copies there. SEC hosts apply their own retention to server logs under their policies.

9. Security

Smart Money uses Android app sandboxing and app-private storage, HTTPS-only traffic (cleartext disabled), disabled backup and data extraction, and temporary content-URI permissions for shared snapshots. Because there is no backend, there is no central database to breach.

10. Children

Smart Money is a financial-information tool directed at adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
