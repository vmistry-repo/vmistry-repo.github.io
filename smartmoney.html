Smart Money:
Insider Tracker

Make public market disclosures easier to understand. Explore SEC EDGAR filings, reported insider transactions, institutional portfolios and company financials — with links to the original sources.

Reported insider trades

Read Form 4 and amended filings with transaction summaries, reported share counts, prices, ownership details and footnotes where available.

🐋

Institutional portfolios

Explore available 13F manager portfolios and compare reported holdings across quarters. These are historical quarter-end disclosures, not live trading positions.

🔔

Following & alerts

Find companies by name or ticker, follow companies and managers, and configure filing alerts. Background checks depend on connectivity, Android scheduling, notification permission and SEC availability.

💬

Filings in context

Browse recent filings across form types, filter by category, and read specialized Form 4, 8-K and 13F summaries where supported. Open document lists, available previews and official SEC sources.

📊

Company financials

Review available reported financial figures and charts. Compare supported company metrics such as revenue, profit, assets and payables for selected periods.

🔒

Clear data handling

Your follows, preferences and downloaded reports stay in app-private storage. Setup uses Google sign-in, with an email fallback if it fails. Your selected email is sent to SEC.gov in the contact header for data requests. Read the privacy policy.

Data source & disclaimer: Filing data comes from the public SEC EDGAR system. Smart Money is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. Information may be delayed, incomplete or amended; alerts are not guaranteed or instantaneous. Verify the original filing. The app does not provide investment advice or execute trades.
